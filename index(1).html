Privacy and transparency

Your participation should be informed.

Kasi Gold aims to handle information responsibly and explain how participation works.

Research data

Read the consent and privacy information displayed in the linked survey before submitting responses. Avoid collecting personal details unless they are needed for a clearly explained purpose. Any follow-up contact should be optional and separate from research participation.

Community contact

If you contact Kasi Gold, share only the information needed to respond to your enquiry. Do not send sensitive personal information through public group chats.

Before publication

This is a starter privacy statement, not a complete legal privacy notice. Replace it with a reviewed notice identifying the responsible organisation, contact details, purposes, lawful basis, retention, access/security practices and relevant data-subject rights before collecting personal information.
